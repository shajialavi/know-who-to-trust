Be honest with yourself

You gave them your trust. They gave you a lesson.

Almost everyone reading this has carried one of these moments quietly, without telling anyone how much it hurt.

“I helped them when no one else did. The day I needed them, they were gone.”सबसे ज़्यादा चोट वहीं लगती है, जहाँ सबसे ज़्यादा भरोसा था।
“A partner I believed in took the money, the credit and my confidence with them.”Business is built on people. One wrong person can undo years.
“I keep wondering how I did not see it coming.”You were not careless. No one ever taught you what to look for.
“Some people leave me exhausted, yet I can’t say why.”That feeling is information. Most of us were taught to ignore it.

It was never about bad luck, and it was never because you are “too trusting”.

Trusting is a beautiful quality. The pain begins when we trust without knowing how to observe. We listen to words, while behaviour has been telling the truth all along.

Over six years of working with more than a thousand people, Shaji Alavi found the same pattern in boardrooms, families and friendships. The people who protect their peace are not the suspicious ones. They are the ones who learned to see clearly, and then trust wisely. That skill can be learned, and it begins in two evenings.
